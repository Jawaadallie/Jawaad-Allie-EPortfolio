  <h2>Reflection on Mock Interview Experience (STAR Method)</h2>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Situation:</strong> As part of our work readiness training, I was tasked with recording a mock interview video to prepare for real-world ICT job interviews.</li>
    <li><strong>Task:</strong> I needed to respond clearly to interview questions, demonstrating both my technical problem-solving capabilities and my interpersonal skills within a strict 2-minute timeframe.</li>
    <li><strong>Action:</strong> I prepared by reviewing common technical interview questions, dressing professionally, setting up a clean background, and practicing answers that highlighted my adaptable background in IT support and hardware troubleshooting.</li>
    <li><strong>Result:</strong> I successfully produced a concise and confident video that effectively showcases my readiness to engage in a professional workplace environment, providing me with valuable practice for my future career.</li>
  </ul>
